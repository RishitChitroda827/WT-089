Frequently Asked Questions

Click on any question below to expand or collapse the answer.

What is the StudentHub Portal?

StudentHub is the campus web application for students and faculty of Faculty of Technology & Engineering (FTE) at CHARUSAT.

How do I register for campus hackathons and technical events?

Navigate to the Events tab, select the event, and register with your student credentials.

How does the theme switcher work?

The switcher modifies the dark-mode CSS class on the body and saves the user's choice using browser localStorage.

Who do I contact for placement and training cell support?

You can visit the Contact page and submit a message, or email placement@charusat.edu.in directly.
